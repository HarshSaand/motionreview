Upload a recorded pose sequence. Inspect a half-second forecast and compare it with simple baselines.

1. Load a motion recording

Use MotionBuilder-named BVH joints or the 17-joint JSON format. Positions can use any consistent length unit; the tool normalises by observed body scale. Files stay on this local machine.

Supported inputs and scope

15–20,000 frames, 15–240 Hz, maximum 20 MiB. JSON must contain fps, joint_names and positions shaped [frames,17,3]. BVH uses the named hips, legs, spine, head and arms. This tool consumes recorded poses; it does not estimate pose from a video. The trained model is a bounded locomotion pilot.

Leave blank to forecast beyond the recording. Choose an earlier frame to compare the forecast with the observed future. Only the preceding 15 frames enter the model.

Interpretation

Forecasts are root-relative and do not predict global travel. Bone lengths may change; inspect the distortion indicator. Reference motion and predictions are not force estimates, collision guarantees or robot control commands. The MLP is always shown alongside persistence and velocity baselines.
